whisper.cpp — bundled runtime (optional)
=======================================

When the build script succeeds in fetching it, Clipwright Studio ships
whisper-cli.exe (plus its runtime DLLs) to enable fully offline transcription.

Source:  https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp
Licence: MIT (Copyright © 2023-2024 The ggml authors)

Speech models (ggml-*.bin) are NOT bundled — the user downloads them from
Hugging Face on demand through Settings → Local models, under the licence of the
respective model (OpenAI Whisper weights, MIT).

If the binary is absent, the application reports offline transcription as
unavailable rather than implying it works.
